Jesper Haga

Associate Professor (Tenure Track) — Foundation Bergsrådet Marcus Wallenberg Associate Professor in Finance

Head, Department of Finance and Economics · Hanken School of Economics

My empirical research centers on financial reporting and corporate governance — in particular the benefits and costs of more frequent financial reporting (quarterly versus semi-annual) for listed companies. Related work looks at cost behavior, earnings management, and organizational resilience. I've also contributed to government-funded research on obstacles to women's career advancement into corporate leadership. edit this paragraph
